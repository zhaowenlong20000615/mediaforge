# Design Context
- Target audience: solo creators, editors, operations teams, and self-hosters processing mixed media locally.
- Core use cases: turn messy batches of video, audio, subtitle, PDF, Word, and image files into verified outputs with recoverable jobs.
- Brand personality: calm industrial studio; precise, tactile, and quietly confident.
- Direction: “workbench at dusk” — warm paper neutrals, ink navy, vermilion status accents, dense but breathable utility layout.
